# F Section · REC Trichy, Class of 1996

We are the F Section group of the 1992–96 batch at REC Trichy (now NIT Trichy).

For four years we ran **Arivu Malar** (அறிவு மலர்), a hand-written monthly magazine. Thirty years on, this is where we keep our photos, videos and magazines from college, the 2016 reunion and the 2026 reunion, in English and Tamil.
